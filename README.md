# GeoFlex HR Analytics Review

## Workforce Findings & Strategic Recommendations

An HR analytics project examining workforce composition, talent acquisition,
employee development, performance, attrition, and branch-level performance
across Nigeria and Kenya from 2019–2025.

---

## Business Context

GeoFlex Workforce Solutions wanted to understand:

- Where workforce challenges are concentrated
- Which recruitment sources perform best
- What factors are associated with employee attrition
- Which branches demonstrate stronger performance
- What actions management should prioritize

---

## Business Questions

1. Which departments need immediate attention?
2. Which branches are performing best?
3. Which recruitment sources deserve more investment?
4. What is driving attrition?

---

## Key Findings

### Workforce

- Total employees: 2,500
- Nigeria accounts for 63.8% of the workforce
- Headcount grew 16.8% year-over-year
- Attrition rate: 14.48%

### Talent Acquisition

- Campus Recruitment recorded a 17.8% hire rate
- Company Website recorded a 17.3% hire rate
- Indeed recorded a 12.5% hire rate
- Job Portal recorded a 14.1% hire rate

### Development & Performance

- Training completion rate: 60.8%
- Only 1.25% of reviews fall within the High performance band
- Average companywide performance score: 75.08/150

### Attrition

- Customer Service had the highest attrition rate at 18.49%
- Administration followed at 17.36%
- Contract employees recorded 16.08% attrition
- Full-Time employees recorded 12.15% attrition
- Relocation and termination were among the leading exit reasons

### Branch Performance

- Sokoto recorded the highest average performance score
- Abeokuta and Abuja Regional Office also ranked among the leading branches
- Benin, Asaba and Makurdi recorded the highest absence rates

---

## Recommendations

1. Stabilise high-attrition departments
2. Fix the early-tenure drop-off
3. Close the Contract vs Full-Time gap
4. Reallocate recruitment spend
5. Recalibrate performance reviews

---

## Data Quality & Governance

The analysis identified data-governance issues in the source data.

Examples include:

- Active Employees showing 400 against a 2,500 headcount
- Duplicate records
- Missing values
- Relational validation issues

Therefore, directional findings are treated as more reliable than exact
figures until the dataset is fully validated.
